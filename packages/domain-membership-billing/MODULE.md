## Invariants

- One subscription row per member, one per provider subscription — database uniques (`packages/database/src/schema/subscriptions.ts:78-79`; `A/modules/data-layer.md:126`).
- Subscription status is one of six values — database enum (`schema/enums.ts:16-23`, `schema/subscriptions.ts:31`).
- A subscription refers to an existing plan — **partly**: `plan_key` has a nullable FK, `plan_id` is NOT NULL free text with no FK (`schema/subscriptions.ts:32-33`).
- Provider event order fields are both set or both null — database CHECK (`schema/subscriptions.ts:87-90`).
- A member has exactly one live membership — **NOT ENFORCED**: a second checkout by a paying member is not prevented (BIL-05, S1). WP-07 completion line 2 requires a database constraint (`docs/engineering/DIRECTION.md:226`).
- A membership ends by date — **NOT ENFORCED**: access is decided from the status only, and nothing ends a membership by date unless a provider event arrives (BIL-06, S1; `packages/domain-membership-billing/src/subscription/lifecycle-reporting.ts:52-83`, cited by `docs/engineering/DIRECTION.md:73`).
- An active membership rests on a verified payment — **NOT ENFORCED** for the company path: an approval may carry no invoice reference, and provisioning writes a `subscriptions` row with `status: 'active'` for a company-covered member without one (`src/sponsors/provisioning-effects.ts:71-91`, confirmed at those lines; `src/sponsors/decisions.ts:32-35,85-94`; `docs/engineering/DIRECTION.md:75`). WP-07 completion line 3 makes a negative test of it.
- An inbound provider event is applied exactly once and in order — **partly**: per-transaction uniqueness admits only the first `transaction.*` event (BIL-03), an interrupted handler leaves an unreclaimed lease for 5 of 7 handled types (BIL-02), and untyped failures are stored as terminal and never re-applied (BIL-01).
- Money is held in whole minor units — **NOT ENFORCED**: invoice and ledger tables are write-only and hold minor-unit strings in `decimal(12,2)` (BIL-09).
- The sponsor mail outbox is written once per (entry, kind) — enforced by an idempotency key with `onConflictDoNothing` (`src/sponsors/provisioning-effects.ts:60-68`). This is the delivery-record pattern the reuse catalogue points at.

### PR-1a — Rates, money and calendar

`Money` holds bigint minor units and a currency code. `toMkdMinor(priceMinor, rate)` returns `round(priceMinor * rate / 100) * 100`, rounding to whole denars half away from zero (Q2). `skopjeDate(instant)` and `addMembershipYear(startsOn)` live in `packages/domain-membership-billing/src/port/calendar.ts` and compute Europe/Skopje business dates (`effective_on`, `week_starts_on`) only, with exports limited to the port's needs and no display formatting or new workspace dependency.

- **`recordExchangeRate`** — WP-02 staff action with role basis `tenant_admin`. Records `caller_kind = 'staff'` and `stored_by` from the session actor. Strict input `{ base, quote, rate, effectiveOn, correctsRateId?, correctionReason? }`, with `rate` a decimal string of up to eight places. Codes `invalid`, `conflict` (duplicate weekly revision or superseded predecessor), `forbidden`.
- **`recordExchangeRateScheduled`** — requires an `IngressCapability<'inngest'>` minted by the Inngest function handler after signature verification. Parses the payload and captures its tenant ID synchronously. Requires a string `tenantId` and obtains `scheduled-work` authority through `authorizeInngestEventTenant(ingress, tenantId)` before reporting an invalid payload or publishing the parsed snapshot. The helper verifies the Inngest capability and refuses an empty or unknown tenant. R-17 requires `tenantId` to come only from the Inngest event payload or a server-side tenant list, never a literal or default. A test must verify refusal of both empty and unknown tenants. Records `caller_kind = 'inngest'` and `stored_by = null`. Strict input `{ tenantId, base, quote, rate, effectiveOn }` excludes corrections. Codes `invalid`, `conflict` (duplicate weekly revision), `forbidden` (including a missing or non-string tenant ID, session, cron capability or supplied `scheduled-work` authority).

Both entries use one private body. A missing book returns `not_found`. The staff action records it as a concealed WP-02 refusal and records `forbidden` as a WP-02 refusal. The `invalid` and `conflict` business failures produce no refusal record. The first publication in an ISO week is revision 1. Its actual `effective_on` still governs when it takes effect.

- **`exchange_rate_books`** — append-only EUR→MKD books. Required `base` and `quote` are constrained to that pair. Unique per `(tenant_id, base, quote)`, with `(tenant_id, id, base, quote)` as FK target. An `AFTER INSERT` trigger on `legal_entities` ensures one book per tenant with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`. The migration backfills and verifies one book for each tenant with an entity. Rate writes and sales fail closed if the book is missing.
- **`exchange_rates`** — append-only with no `TRUNCATE` grant. `book_id`, `base`, `quote`, `rate`, `effective_on`, `week_starts_on`, `revision`, `source`, `caller_kind` and `stored_at` are `NOT NULL`. The `numeric(18,8)` rate is positive and excludes `NaN`. `effective_on` is finite and `source = 'nbrm'`. The plain `week_starts_on` column is the ISO-week Monday, including year boundaries. The named CHECK `exchange_rates_week_starts_on_iso_monday` requires `week_starts_on = effective_on - (extract(isodow from effective_on)::int - 1)`. The book FK fixes tenant and currency pair. `(tenant_id, book_id, week_starts_on, revision)` is unique. The correction FK to `(tenant_id, id, book_id, effective_on)` preserves the predecessor's book and actual date. The sale's rate FK also fixes the pair and rate. A CHECK limits `caller_kind` to `staff` or `inngest`. A CHECK requires `stored_by` exactly for staff. Its composite FK references a user of that tenant.
- **Rate insert guard** — invoker with fixed `search_path`. Locks the book `FOR UPDATE`, refuses a supplied non-null `week_starts_on` that differs from the ISO Monday derived from `effective_on`, sets the derived value, and stamps `stored_at = clock_timestamp()` after the lock. Revisions start at 1, with null predecessor and reason exactly on the first revision. Corrections are staff-only and name the greatest revision in the same book and ISO week, increment it by one, and require a reason with a non-whitespace character (`correction_reason ~ '\S'`, R9-6).
- **Rate in force at `t`** — a session with different home and access tenants receives `forbidden` before a transaction opens. The greatest `effective_on` and then greatest `revision` wins among rows with `stored_at <= t` and `effective_on <= skopjeDate(t)`. No eligible row gives `rate_unavailable`.

The `./payment-port` export exposes the staff command and its strict schema, the scheduled command, `getExchangeRateInForce`, `Money` and `toMkdMinor` in PR-1a. The staff action passes the home tenant and actor from the WP-02 wrapper's authenticated session.

The PR-1a migration registers its new SQL-only functions and triggers in `scripts/ci/sql-managed-objects.json` (R-12 registry), new objects only.

PR-1a tests require refusal of an otherwise valid direct insert with a wrong `week_starts_on` and refusal of injected `stored_by` or `caller_kind` in either rate entry's strict schema. Direct UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE are refused under the restricted role (R-06).

### PR-1b — Individual sales

All five entries use the session actor.

- **`publishPlanVersion`** — WP-02 role basis `tenant_admin`, strict `{ planId, legalEntityId }`. Locks the plan `FOR UPDATE` and builds the version from the catalogue. Codes `forbidden` for a denied basis and `invalid` for an ineligible version, inactive plan or entity, or missing or blank legal fields.
- **`withdrawPlanVersion`** — role basis `tenant_admin`, strict `{ planVersionId }`. Locks the plan `FOR UPDATE`, then the named version. A retry returns the same withdrawn version. Codes `forbidden` for a denied basis and `not_found` for a missing or out-of-tenant version.
- **`getSaleOffer`** — role basis `agent`, `staff` or `tenant_admin`. Accepts `{ planId }` with no buyer or branch scope. One statement snapshot checks the current individual offer, legal fields and rate in force. Returns `{ planVersionId, listPriceMinor, priceCurrency, legalEntityId, termsVersion, rateId, rateEffectiveOn, amountMkdMinor }`. Codes `forbidden` for a denied basis, concealed `not_found` for a missing or RLS-invisible plan (R9-5), `stale_offer` for a visible plan with no eligible offer, and `rate_unavailable` for no rate in force or a quote that rounds to zero or below.
- **`recordSale`** and **`recordSaleInTx`** — same role basis as `getSaleOffer` plus buyer scope. Agents may sell to individual leads assigned to them in their branch. Staff may sell to individual leads and members in their branch. Tenant admins may sell to any individual lead or member of their tenant. Strict input `{ buyer, planVersionId, termsVersion, rateId, amountMkdMinor, channel, operationKey }` uses the accepted quote and exactly one of `buyer.leadId` or `buyer.memberUserId`. `recordSale` uses the wrapper and opens its tenant transaction. `recordSaleInTx` uses the caller's tenant transaction. Codes `forbidden` for a denied basis, `not_found` for a missing or out-of-scope buyer or replay, `stale_offer`, `stale_rate`, `rate_unavailable`, `conflict` for a different replay hash, and `invalid` for invalid input or isolation.

PR-1b actions map `{ok:false}` to WP-02's branded `actionFailure`. `not_found` and `forbidden` use the refusal catalogue and create refusal records. `stale_offer`, `stale_rate`, `rate_unavailable`, `conflict` and `invalid` carry business codes and produce no refusal record.

- **`membership_plan_versions`** — common catalogue-version identity. Annual, EUR, positive-price versions exclude `family` and may include `business`. All columns are required except the withdrawal group. Tenant-scoped FKs tie the version to the plan, entity, publisher and withdrawer. Versions are unique by `(tenant_id, plan_id, version)`. The sale FK targets the version identity with its plan, entity, price, currency, tier and member count.
- **Version guards** — `publishPlanVersion` copies the catalogue tier, interval, member count and currency and derives the exact minor-unit price from the stored plan price. On every insert, the trigger locks the plan `FOR UPDATE` and the entity `FOR SHARE` and independently refuses the insert unless both are active, the supplied tier, interval, member count and currency equal the locked plan's values, `list_price_minor = price * 100` exactly using the locked plan's stored `price`, and `version = max + 1`. The withdrawal group starts null and permits one complete update of `withdrawn_at` and `withdrawn_by`, with the plan locked `FOR UPDATE`. Other updates and deletes are refused. The current offer is the greatest non-withdrawn version while its plan and entity are active. Withdrawal of the newest exposes the previous non-withdrawn version, if any.
- **Legal fields** — publication, quote and sale guard all require non-null `governing_law` and `terms_version` that pass `btrim(…, E' \t\r\n') <> ''` (R9-3). The sale snapshots both from the locked entity.
- **`membership_sale_kinds`** and **`membership_sale_channels`** — written only by migrations, with runtime `SELECT` only. The kind table has a text PK and one row, `individual`. The channel table has PK `(kind, channel)`, an FK to the kind and two rows, `(individual, cash)` and `(individual, bank_transfer)`. Both enable and force RLS. `SELECT` requires `nullif(btrim(current_setting('app.current_access_tenant_id', true)), '') is not null` and shows the same keys to every initialized access tenant.
- **`membership_sales`** — common columns are `NOT NULL` except the reserved void group. Required `kind` and `(kind, channel)` reference the lookups. Tenant-scoped FKs bind the claim hash, complete rate and individual plan version snapshots, buyer, seller, attribution and void actor to their rows. Positive prices and amounts satisfy the exact whole-denar conversion, `amount_mkd_minor = round(price_minor * rate / 100) * 100` (`price_minor = list_price_minor` for individual sales). The port helper and the CHECK use an exact product and division in unbounded `numeric` or exact scaled-integer arithmetic, with no scale-limited intermediate (R9-1). For individual sales, kind-scoped CHECKs require non-null `list_price_minor`, `plan_tier` and `plan_members_included`. They also require exactly one buyer of the declared kind, full offer price, `due_on = sold_on` and tier `basic` or `standard` with one included member. Unique `(tenant_id, operation_key)`, unique `(tenant_id, id, amount_mkd_minor)` for the charge FK, and one live sale per tenant and lead where `lead_id` is present and `voided_at` is null.
- **Reserved void group** — `voided_at`, `voided_by` and `void_reason` start null. The state guard permits one complete null-to-value transition and refuses other updates and deletes. The PR-1b runtime role has no sale `UPDATE` privilege.
- **Individual sale insert guard** — invoker with fixed `search_path`, scoped to `NEW.kind = 'individual'`. The trigger requires `current_setting('transaction_isolation') = 'read committed'` (R9-2). Locks the rate book, plan, legal entity and buyer `FOR SHARE` in that order. Sets `sold_at = clock_timestamp()` and `sold_on = skopjeDate(sold_at)` and verifies the current eligible offer and copied legal fields (`stale_offer`) and the rate in force (`stale_rate`). The buyer must be a same-tenant individual lead or a same-tenant user with role `member`. Attribution matches the lead's agent and branch or the member's branch with a null agent. The guard recomputes the claim digest from the sale and refuses missing fields or a mismatch.
- **`membership_sale_claims`** — append-only. Required `tenant_id`, `operation_key`, `request_hash` and `claimed_at`. PK `(tenant_id, operation_key)`, with unique `(tenant_id, operation_key, request_hash)` as the sale FK target. The hash is 32-byte SHA-256 (`bytea`). Canonical fields in order are `individual:v1`, buyer kind, buyer ID, plan version ID, terms version, rate ID, MKD minor units and channel. Prefix each field's exact UTF-8 bytes with its four-byte unsigned big-endian byte length. Encode the amount as a base-10 integer with no plus sign or leading zeroes. The claim and insert guard use the same database hash function.
- **Claim and replay** — both sale entries require `READ COMMITTED`, else `invalid`, and propagate serialization failures to the transaction owner. After checking the basis, the command opens a savepoint and checks the requested buyer's scope before making the claim its first data write. The claim uses `INSERT … ON CONFLICT (tenant_id, operation_key) DO NOTHING RETURNING` and waits on a concurrent claim. On replay, a fresh statement reads the original sale and locks its buyer `FOR SHARE`. Missing sale or buyer outside current scope gives `not_found` before any hash comparison. A different hash gives `conflict`, and the same hash returns the original before offer or rate revalidation. A new claim takes the sale guard's locks and rechecks buyer scope before sale validation and insert.
- **Savepoint and commit** — on any failure the command rolls back to and releases the savepoint before returning or throwing. Success releases it. A `DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED` constraint trigger requires a sale per claim at commit. The composed command checks that the caller left the claim constraint `DEFERRED` or sets it `DEFERRED` inside the savepoint (R9-4).
- **`membership_ledger_entries`** — append-only, `charge` only. An `AFTER INSERT` trigger on the sale posts its matching charge in the same transaction. FK `(tenant_id, sale_id, amount_mkd_minor)` ties the charge to the sale and amount. Unique `(tenant_id, sale_id)` where `kind = 'charge'` and a `DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED` constraint trigger require exactly one matching charge per sale at commit.

PR-1b tests require the strict schemas to refuse injected `sold_by` in either sale entry and `published_by` or `publishedBy` in `publishPlanVersion`. Direct writes to the kind and channel lookups and direct sale UPDATEs are refused under the restricted role (R-06).
