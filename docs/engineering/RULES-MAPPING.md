## Transition clause

Drafting is not adoption. Nothing in this file removes an existing check.
- A mandatory gate stays in effect until a specific replacement or removal meets the A5 conditions and the owner approves it. Part 3 states that transition for each gate.
- A `RETIRED` or `REPLACED` instruction line stays in the live `AGENTS.md` until the owner adopts the new file.
- Replacing a file never drops an `UNREVIEWED` row: those rows stay in force through the opening paragraph of the proposed `AGENTS.md` until they are reviewed.
