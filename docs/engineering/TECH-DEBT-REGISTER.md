## How to read it

- **A row is one piece of debt:** usually one finding; a measurement without a finding where R1 or R2 measured one. Business gates, missing pilot features and owner questions are not debt and are not here: they are work packages and questions of `DIRECTION.md`.
- **Decision.** *Before any new feature*: a package of group 1 resolves it (WP-01 to WP-06). *Before the pilot*: a package of group 2 (WP-07 to WP-19). *Later*: group 3, or code of the parked payment provider, or no package. *Accept*: keeping costs less than fixing. *Replaced by the redesign*: the only location is a screen that the rebuild replaces; listed once, not ranked.
- **The decision says when the debt is paid, not whether the pilot waits for it.** What blocks the pilot is `DIRECTION.md`, part 6.2 and part 6.6.
- **Order.** By decision; inside a decision by the severity of the finding, which follows demonstrated consequences, then by effort; rows without a finding come last in their group.
- **`RUN C`** marks a row whose cost needs a new measurement (unused files and exports, a typed import graph, step times in CI, the cost of the local verification). **`LEAD, NOT VERIFIED`** marks a claim of a memory note that nobody checked against the code: the first action for such a row is to verify it.
- **Effort** is the record's: S, M, L, XL. Hours are `NOT MEASURED` everywhere.
