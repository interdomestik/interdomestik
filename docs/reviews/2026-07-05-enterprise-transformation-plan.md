## Maturity Scale

- **M1 — Ad-hoc:** capability exists informally, no evidence discipline.
- **M2 — Governed pilot:** capability enforced by process/gates, evidence exists
  but is scattered. Interdomestik's modal level today is M2+.
- **M3 — Production:** capability proven by consolidated, dated evidence;
  failure paths rehearsed; a stranger could verify it in an afternoon.
- **M4 — Enterprise:** independently verifiable, scales beyond the founding
  team, and survives personnel change.
- **M5 — Audited enterprise:** third-party attestation, continuous verification,
  and audit-ready operating cadence.

## Maturity Stages

- **Stage 0 — Governed Pilot (today).** M0→M5 architecture closed, gate loop
  proven by MOB-01, dark surfaces, no paid public exposure.
- **Stage 1 — Production (paid launch).** Real members pay; every promise on a
  paying surface is validated; all launch-mandatory capabilities at M3. Target:
  ~90 days.
- **Stage 2 — Enterprise (partner/sponsor/customer scale).** Sponsors, partner
  lawyers, group deals, >1k members, multi-country live; enterprise-mandatory
  capabilities at M3-M4. Target: ~6 months.
- **Stage 3 — Audited Enterprise.** External pen test passed, control register
  audit-ready, DR rehearsed on cadence, SLOs enforced. Target: ~12 months.

## Operating Thesis

This is not mainly a remediation story. Interdomestik already has unusually
strong governance, boundary discipline, and evidence for its age. The work is
to consolidate scattered proof into registers, rehearse the failure paths the
documents already describe, validate externally once the product surface is
stable, and let the commercial track fund the cadence.

Nothing in this plan requires re-architecture. Everything requires
follow-through.
