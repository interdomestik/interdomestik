## Acceptance Vocabulary

| Decision                  | Meaning                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `accepted`                | Evidence is complete enough for the named step, but does not imply launch unless the step is itself a launch authority record |
| `returned_for_correction` | Evidence exists but cannot be accepted until named fields or artifacts are fixed                                              |
| `blocked`                 | Evidence proves the step cannot proceed; next current-authority discussion must cite the blocker                              |
| `needs_instrumentation`   | Existing provider/system cannot prove the required signal; a later authorized instrumentation slice is needed                 |
| `superseded`              | A later dated correction replaced this evidence row                                                                           |
