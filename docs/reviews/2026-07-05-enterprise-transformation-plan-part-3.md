# Enterprise Transformation Plan — Roadmap And Investment Order

> Status: Transformation-plan appendix, docs-only input. This creates no
> runtime authority.

## Phased Roadmap

**Phase A — Now to 30 days:** decisions and evidence, almost no code. Run B2,
sign memos, secure L2-KS, write B6, confirm `/help-now` alerts and rota, create
processor and findings registers, write tm10 and RC checklist, produce first
reconciliation report, codify two-approval rule, and run MOB-01b only through
authority. Theme: five signatures and six documents.

**Phase B — 30 to 90 days:** production paid launch. Run MOB-05a, MOB-02, and
MOB-03(car/property) through gates; consolidate tenant-isolation proof; add
role-session coverage; rehearse DSR, refund, rollback, and game-day paths; ship
status page and 10 runbooks; advance L1/L5/DPIA; review expansion criteria at
day 90. Theme: member spine live and every promise validated.

**Phase C — 3 to 6 months:** enterprise/partner scale. Run MOB-05b ceremony
after L1, put sponsor kit into market, load test and degradation drill, pen test
and remediate, complete log-PII audit and retention schedule, prove AI cadence,
add boundary lint, and promote WS-F/OMG only if commercially justified. Theme:
external parties can verify the platform.

**Phase D — 6 to 12 months:** audited enterprise. Maintain audit-consumable
control/evidence register, expand pen-test scope, rehearse DR on cadence,
execute DOM packaging when scale demands, and decide on certification only when
customers demand it.

## Mandatory Before Paid Launch

B2 record; L2 for every non-dark country; Memo 1 plus L5 fee lines;
review-SLA validation; imprint page; B6 content-hotfix runbook; `/help-now`
alert coverage and on-call rota; first Paddle reconciliation report; refund
path E2E; KPI dashboard live; RC checklist in use.

## Mandatory Before Enterprise / Partner Scale

Consolidated tenant-isolation proof; role-session coverage on all four
surfaces; pen test with highs closed; processor/DPA register; retention
schedule; DSR rehearsal; DPIA for medical scope; L1 matrix and ceremony live;
10x load test with SLO headroom; 10 runbooks and game-day record; status page;
customer-notice procedure; sponsor privacy negative tests; two-approval rule;
succession note; monthly reconciliation with clean deltas.

## Optional Polish

Dark mode, RTL, native-app split, expert marketplace, court-path UI, sponsor
self-service portal, proactive VONESA scanning, boundary-lint automation beyond
quarterly snapshots, certification without customer demand, dashboard aesthetics
beyond the five KPIs.

## Safest Order Of Investment

1. Verify what exists: B2, findings register, isolation-proof compilation.
2. Make decisions: memos, L5, PWA owner.
3. Consolidate evidence: registers, runbooks, reports.
4. Open the commercial faucet: L2, MOB-01b, dashboard.
5. Rehearse operations: drills, DSR, rollback, reconciliation.
6. Build the member wave: MOB-05a, MOB-02, MOB-03, MOB-05b.
7. Validate externally: pen test and load test once stable.
8. Scale deliberately: DOM, WS-F, OMG, certification only against demand.

## Top Enterprise-Grade Gaps

1. No consolidated control/evidence register.
2. No independent penetration test.
3. B2 unverified or, after reproduction, RBAC-01 not yet proven on staging.
4. No on-call rota.
5. RLS/isolation proof scattered.
6. No DR/BCP with stated RTO/RPO cadence.
7. Processor/DPA register absent.
8. Finance reconstruction undemonstrated.
9. No load/performance evidence at volume.
10. Founder-dependent governance.
