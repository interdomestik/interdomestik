## Decision To Record

When Interdomestik approves a third-party expert or court cost and recovery
fails, decide whether the member owes that cost.

## Decider

- Accountable decider: Arben Lila unless delegated by name.
- Consulted before signature: finance and counsel / L5 owner.
- Required before: MOB-05a Fee Math Sheet promotion and L5 `fees.*` wording.

## Selected Option

Mark exactly one:

- [ ] A — Interdomestik absorbs approved expert/court costs on lost cases.
- [ ] B — Success fee is waived, but approved third-party costs remain
      member-payable on loss.
- [ ] C — Interdomestik absorbs approved costs up to a cap; above-cap amounts
      are member-approved as at-risk.
